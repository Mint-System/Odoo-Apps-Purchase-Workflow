Prepare Purchase-To-Order:

- In (General) Settings > Inventory activate Multi-Step Routes (don't forget to save).
- Open App Inventory > Configuration > Warehouse Management > Routes.
- Active the "Archive" filter.
- Chose "Replenish on Order (MTO)".
- Perform "Unarchive" Action.
- Open or create product.
- Switch to the "Inventory" tab and activate "Replenish on Order (MTO)".

Prepare Traceability:

- Go to Settings > Inventory > Traceability and activate "Consignment".

Prepare Client:

- Install the "Sales" app.
- Create client for sale (has to be different from supplier of product).

Sale product and check purchase to order:

- Create sales order to the sale client for the prepared product.
- Switch to the generated purchase order. 
- Click "Confirm Order".
- Check if picking belonging to purchase has sale client as "Assign Owner"
